Radiological Differentiation Criteria

Glioma / GBM

Intra-axial, thick irregular peripheral ring enhancement with central hypo-intensity representing coagulative necrosis. Extensive surrounding vasogenic edema extending along white matter tracts.

Meningioma

Extra-axial, dural-based mass arising along convexities or falx cerebri. Intense, uniform contrast enhancement with hallmark "dural tail sign" and CSF cleft sign separating from brain parenchyma.

Pituitary Adenoma

Sellar and suprasellar mass expanding from the hypophysis. Characteristic "snowman" or "figure-8" appearance due to indentation by diaphragma sellae with optic chiasm elevation.
